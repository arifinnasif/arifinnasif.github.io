An AI powered client-to-client e-commerce platform for posting ads to buy or sell. Phone and email verification, payment gateways, live chats and sponsored search are some of many features. I contributed in developing <strong>TradeSpaceAI, a three stage fraud detection AI</strong> that could help a human reviewer whether an ad should be allowed to be published on the platform. We strictly maintained type safety for a clean and readable codebase. <strong>This project received the Departmental Best Project Award from CSE, BUET</strong>
